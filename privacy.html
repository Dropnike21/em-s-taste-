CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy at Em’s Taste

Pre-launch notice: This page describes the current website's intended data handling. The business owner must add a working privacy contact, verify retention procedures, and review applicable legal obligations before opening orders to the public.

Information we collect

When you sign in using Google, the site uses Firebase Authentication to identify your account. It stores your Firebase user ID, display name, email address, and the date you first joined. We do not receive or store your Google password.

When you request a meal, we collect the name and workplace/pickup group you enter, dishes, quantities, prices, notes you provide, time submitted, and order status. For bulk meal requests (over 50 servings), we store requested dates, meal preferences, quantities, quotation and staff-review status. We also store requested menu additions, feedback categories, optional ratings, bug/issue reports, report status, and any details you submit. Completed-order ratings are stored with their related order.

How we use information

We use this information to process and review meal requests, prevent repeated submissions for the same workday, organize meals by workplace, handle feedback, respond to support requests, and understand aggregate menu preferences. Staff may block accounts associated with misuse. Google authentication alone does not verify that every order is genuine.

Who can access it

Authorized administrators can access customer profiles, private order details, and customer reports. Approved staff can access food menu management, order and bulk booking details, and menu suggestions, but do not have access to the full customer directory or general issue reports. Customers can view their own requests, order history and ratings. Visitors can see the published menu. Firebase (Google) supplies authentication and database infrastructure.

Storage and security

Personal data is stored in Firebase services and access is restricted through Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not include sensitive information in order notes or feedback.

Your choices and requests

You can stop using the website and sign out at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion of stored data, contact the Em’s Taste business owner through your participating workplace coordinator until a dedicated privacy contact method is published. Account sign-out alone does not delete existing orders or reviews.

Retention

Orders and reviews are retained until the business sets and applies an appropriate retention/deletion schedule. That schedule must be finalized before public launch, and this notice updated accordingly.
